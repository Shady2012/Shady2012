# Hi there, I'm Shadrack O. Aboagye 👋

**Research Associate | Smart Electronics | Wearable Electrical Impedance Tomography (EIT)**

I build wearable EIT systems that detect breast cancer early, and I design them to work for women that mainstream diagnostics overlook. My PhD at Nottingham Trent University produced the "Smart Bra", a smart textile platform that pairs a hybrid embroidery-carbon electrode system with custom EIDORS algorithms for automated tumour localisation. I work across sensor design, precision analogue hardware, and inverse problem algorithms, and I translate physiological signals into clinical data.

I lead the Wearable Technology Lab in the Advanced Textiles Research Group (ATRG) and consult on electronics engineering at the Medical Technologies Innovation Facility (MTIF). The Smart Bra work reached the UK Department of Health and Social Care and received a commendation to the National Screening Committee.

### 🔬 Current Research & Expertise
* **Hardware Engineering:** I design modular, high-fidelity PCB stacks for data acquisition, including custom Voltage-Controlled Current Sources (Howland Pumps) and Adaptive Gain Control systems.
* **Smart Textiles:** I fabricate and characterise skin-safe, dry-contact electrodes (carbon-silicone composites and embroidered silver yarn) for wearable bioimpedance.
* **Computational Imaging:** I implement Gauss-Newton solvers and custom regularisations (NOSER, Laplacian) in the EIDORS framework to reconstruct 3D conductivity maps.
* **Clinical Translation:** I take bench-top phantom validation through to clinical feasibility protocols and regulatory compliance (IEC 60601-1), working with clinical collaborators.
* **Inclusive Design:** I test sensors across skin types and user groups so that performance holds for the women who need screening most.
